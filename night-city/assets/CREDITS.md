## Trees (city)

`trees/` holds real-time versions of three scanned Poly Haven trees, made with
`tools/reduce_tree.py` (Blender) from the original glTF files:

- `street_tree` ← tree_small_02
- `park_tree` ← jacaranda_tree
- `bush_tree` ← island_tree_02
